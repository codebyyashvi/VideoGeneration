## 🚫 CRITICAL COMPLIANCE RULES

### Rule #1: NOT an Insurance Company
LayoffShield is **NOT an insurance company** and must **NEVER** be positioned, marketed, or represented as such. This is the highest priority compliance requirement.

### Rule #2: Forbidden Terminology
**NEVER use these terms in video content:**
- insurance
- insurance company
- insurer  
- insurance product
- insurance benefit
- claim
- premium
- coverage
- guaranteed payout
- assured protection
- insurance protection

### Rule #3: Mandatory Alternative Terms
When you need to reference these concepts, use the approved alternatives:

| ❌ Forbidden | ✅ Use Instead |
|---|---|
| insurance | membership or career support |
| insurance product | career support program |
| claim | request |
| premium | subscription fee |
| coverage | protection package |
| guaranteed payout | discretionary support |
| insurance benefit | member benefit |
| insurance company | LayoffShield |
| assured protection | career readiness tools |

### Rule #4: Financial Support Framing
When mentioning financial support, ALWAYS frame it this way:
- ✅ "Discretionary support" (NOT guaranteed)
- ✅ "At company discretion" (NOT automatic)
- ✅ "May provide" (NOT "will provide")
- ✅ "Available to eligible members at LayoffShield's discretion" (NOT guaranteed)

## ⚠️ KEY RISKS TO AVOID

### Implementation Risk
If videos consistently make financial support guarantees or promises, regulatory scrutiny may increase. Always keep messaging aligned with Terms & Conditions.

### Consumer Perception Risk
If videos create an expectation of "assured financial protection," they may contradict marketing disclaimers. Avoid any imagery or language that suggests a safety net.

### Regulatory Risk
Using insurance terminology or positioning LayoffShield as insurance could:
- Trigger IRDAI regulatory review
- Result in compliance violations
- Damage brand credibility
- Lead to legal action

### Brand Alignment Risk
Fear-based messaging or overpromising damages the brand's trustworthy, professional image.
